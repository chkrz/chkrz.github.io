About

I'm Hui Cai. I work at the intersection of large language models and real-world systems -- training models, building multi-agent frameworks, and shipping AI-powered applications.

My background is in mathematics and quantitative finance. I studied at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (BS) and the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor (MS). Before moving into AI, I worked in quant research and data analytics, which is probably why I still think in probabilities.

Recently I've been exploring Polymarket and thinking about how AI changes the way we work -- not just what we build, but how we manage the building process itself.
