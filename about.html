About

I'm Hui Cai. I'm an algorithm engineer at StepFun, focused on LLM post-training for finance, automated alpha mining, and building benchmarks that measure how well language models understand financial markets.

My background is in mathematics and quantitative finance. I studied at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (BS) and the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor (MS). Before moving into AI, I worked in quant research and data analytics, which is probably why I still think in probabilities.

Two things I care about: using LLMs to automatically mine alpha signals for quantitative strategies, and improving model capabilities in the financial domain through post-training and rigorous benchmarking.
